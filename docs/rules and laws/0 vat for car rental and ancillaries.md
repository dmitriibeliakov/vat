| Aspect              | Treatment                                                  |
| :------------------ | :--------------------------------------------------------- |

---

Car rental is subject to the standard 21% VAT rate in the Netherlands -- it is not listed in Tabel I (reduced) or Tabel II (zero). There are no special provisions or reduced rates for vehicle rental, unlike flights which benefit from the zero rate.

C Teleport acts as a **disclosed agent** (bemiddelaar) for car rental bookings -- mediating in the name and for the account of the car rental company. The rental price is a **doorlopende post** (pass-through) and we only charge VAT on our commission/markup.

---

## 1. Car Rental -- 21% Standard Rate

Car rental is not listed in any reduced-rate or zero-rate table. The standard 21% rate applies under **Artikel 9, lid 1 Wet OB 1968**.

There is **no zero-rate provision** for vehicle rental, even when connected to international business travel. Tabel II post b.3 (international transport) covers the transport service itself, not the rental of means of transport. Tabel II post a.3 covers seagoing vessels and aircraft used in international transport -- not cars or land vehicles.

| Level        | Document                        | Provision                                                    |
| :----------- | :------------------------------ | :----------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Dutch**    | *Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968* | **Art. 9, lid 1** (standard rate 21%)                        |
| **Dutch**    | *Wet OB 1968, Tabel I*         | Car rental **not listed** (no reduced rate)                  |
| **Dutch**    | *Wet OB 1968, Tabel II*        | Car rental **not listed** (no zero rate)                     |
| **European** | *Directive 2006/112/EC*        | **Art. 96-97** (standard rate not less than 15%)             |

**Rate: 21%**

## 2. Place of Supply -- Where Is the Rental Taxed?

Car rental has a **special place-of-supply rule** that depends on the duration of the hire. This differs from most B2B services.

### Short-Term Hire (up to 30 days)

For rentals of **30 days or less**, the place of supply is where the vehicle is **actually put at the disposal** of the customer. This applies regardless of whether the customer is B2B or B2C.

> **Art. 6g, lid 1 Wet OB 1968:** *"De plaats van dienst van kortdurende verhuur van een vervoermiddel is de plaats waar dat vervoermiddel daadwerkelijk ter beschikking van de afnemer wordt gesteld."*
> *(The place of supply of short-term hire of a means of transport is the place where that means of transport is actually put at the disposal of the customer.)*

> **Art. 6g, lid 3 Wet OB 1968:** Defines "kortdurende verhuur" as continuous possession or use of not more than **30 days** (90 days for ships).

**Practical implication:** A car picked up in Spain is taxed in Spain, regardless of whether the customer is Dutch, EU, or non-EU. The car rental company applies Spanish VAT.

### Long-Term Hire (more than 30 days)

For B2B: the general rule applies -- taxed where the customer is established (**Art. 6, lid 1 Wet OB 1968 / Art. 44 Directive**). This scenario is unlikely for typical business travel car rentals.

| Duration   | Customer type | Place of supply                        | Legal Basis                                |
| :--------- | :------------ | :------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------- |
| ≤ 30 days  | B2B or B2C    | Where vehicle put at customer disposal | Art. 6g(1) Wet OB / Art. 56(1) Directive   |
| > 30 days  | B2B           | Where customer is established          | Art. 6(1) Wet OB / Art. 44 Directive       |
| > 30 days  | B2C           | Where customer is established/resides  | Art. 6g(2) Wet OB / Art. 56(2) Directive   |

## 3. Our Role as Disclosed Agent (Bemiddelaar)

C Teleport acts as a **disclosed agent** -- we mediate in the name and for the account of the car rental company. The car rental company makes the supply directly to the customer.

### Rental Price Is a Pass-Through (Doorlopende Post)

The rental price we collect from the customer is a **doorlopende post** -- excluded from our taxable amount. We do not apply VAT to it. The car rental company applies the local VAT of the country where the car is picked up.

| Level        | Document                                 | Provision                                                          |
| :----------- | :--------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Dutch**    | *Uitvoeringsbesluit omzetbelasting 1968* | **Artikel 4** (doorlopende posten excluded from taxable amount)    |
| **Dutch**    | *Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968*          | **Artikel 8** (taxable amount = commission only)                   |
| **European** | *Directive 2006/112/EC*                  | **Article 79(c)** (pass-through excluded from taxable amount)      |

### VAT on Our Commission (B2B)

Our commission/markup is a standard intermediation service. For B2B customers, the **general place-of-supply rule** applies -- taxed where the customer is established:

* **Dutch customer:** 21% Dutch VAT on commission
* **EU customer:** Reverse charge (reported in Box 3B / ICP)
* **Non-EU customer:** Outside scope of Dutch VAT

| Level        | Document                        | Provision                                     |
| :----------- | :------------------------------ | :-------------------------------------------- |
| **Dutch**    | *Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968* | **Art. 6, lid 1** (B2B general rule)          |
| **European** | *Directive 2006/112/EC*        | **Art. 44** (B2B general rule)                |

**Important distinction from flights:** For international passenger transport, intermediation is zero-rated under Tabel II, post b.4. **No equivalent provision exists for car rental intermediation.** The standard 21% rate applies.

**Important distinction from hotels:** For hotels (B2C intermediation), the place of supply follows the location of the underlying service (Art. 6a / Art. 46). For car rental intermediation to B2B customers, the general rule applies instead. The distinction matters because C Teleport primarily serves B2B customers.

### The Buy/Resell Fiction Does NOT Apply

**Art. 4, lid 4 Wet OB 1968** (implementing **Art. 28 Directive 2006/112/EC**) deems an undisclosed agent to have bought and sold the service. As a disclosed agent, we fall outside this fiction.

### TOMS Does NOT Apply

As a disclosed agent, TOMS is explicitly excluded. **Art. 306(1) second subparagraph Directive 2006/112/EC** states that the TOMS scheme "shall not apply to travel agents where they act solely as intermediaries and to whom point (c) of the first paragraph of Article 79 applies" (i.e., the doorlopende post mechanism).

| Level        | Document                        | Provision                                                                               |
| :----------- | :------------------------------ | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Dutch**    | *Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968* | **Art. 28z-28zg** (Reisbureauregeling -- does NOT apply to disclosed agents)            |
| **European** | *Directive 2006/112/EC*        | **Art. 306(1)** (TOMS explicitly excludes intermediaries using Art. 79(c) pass-through) |

## 4. Ancillary Services -- GPS, Child Seats, Additional Driver

Extras like GPS navigation, child seats, additional driver fees, and fuel packages are **ancillary** to the main car rental. Under the ECJ's ancillary service principle, they do not constitute an aim in themselves for the customer but are a means of better enjoying the principal service (the rental).

These follow the same VAT treatment as the main rental: they are part of the car rental company's supply, flow through as doorlopende post, and C Teleport does not apply VAT to them.

| Level        | Document / Case                        | Provision                                              |
| :----------- | :------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------- |
| **European** | *ECJ Case C-349/96 (CPP)*             | Ancillary service shares VAT treatment of principal    |
| **European** | *ECJ Case C-41/04 (Levob)*            | Confirms CPP principles on composite supplies          |

## 5. CDW / Insurance -- Special Treatment

Car rental often includes insurance-like products. The VAT treatment depends on whether it is **genuine insurance** or a **contractual waiver** (CDW).

### CDW (Collision Damage Waiver) -- NOT Insurance

CDW is a contractual waiver by the rental company of its right to charge for damage. It is **not** a genuine insurance product provided by a licensed insurer. Therefore, the Art. 135(1)(a) exemption for insurance **does not apply**. CDW follows the VAT treatment of the car rental (21% standard rate).

### Genuine Insurance -- Exempt, Separate Supply

If actual insurance is arranged through a licensed insurer, it is a **separate supply** exempt from VAT under the insurance exemption. The ECJ ruled in **C-224/11 (BGZ Leasing)** that insurance supplied alongside leasing/rental is "in principle a separate and independent supply, not ancillary to the rental."

| Type              | VAT Treatment                           | Legal Basis                                            |
| :---------------- | :-------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------- |
| CDW / damage waiver | 21% (follows rental rate, not exempt) | ECJ C-224/11 (not genuine insurance)                   |
| Genuine insurance | **Exempt** (separate supply)            | Art. 11(1)(a) Wet OB / Art. 135(1)(a) Directive       |

**For C Teleport as disclosed agent:** Both CDW and insurance charges from the rental company flow through as doorlopende post -- not our concern for VAT purposes. If we separately arranged genuine insurance for a customer (from a licensed insurer), that could be an exempt supply, but would require specific insurance intermediary licensing.
