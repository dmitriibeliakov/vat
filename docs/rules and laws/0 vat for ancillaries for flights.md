Extra baggage is legally treated as an **ancillary service (bijkomende prestatie)** to the transport of persons. Because it is inextricably linked to the flight, it follows the VAT treatment of the passenger transport itself.

International Passenger Transport: Subject to the 0% VAT rate (Table II, post b.3) in the Netherlands, provided the destination or departure is outside the Netherlands. See [0 vat for ancillaties for flights.md]

Baggage: The Dutch Tax Administration (Belastingdienst) explicitly confirms that the 0% rate for international flights also applies to baggage carried by passengers.

As an intermediary, we are essentially "mediating" a service that is legally defined as a single composite supply.

Below is the updated legal basis for treating extra baggage as an ancillary service (bijkomende prestatie) and why it must share the VAT treatment of the main passenger transport.

### 1. The Principle of "Ancillary Services" (EU Legal Base)

Under European VAT law, a service is considered "ancillary" to a principal service if it does not constitute an aim in itself for the customer, but a means of better enjoying the principal service.

* **Legal Reference:** **ECJ Case C-349/96 (Card Protection Plan / CPP)**.
* **The Rule:** The European Court of Justice ruled that where a transaction consists of several elements, there is a **single supply** when one or more elements are to be regarded as the principal service, while other elements are to be regarded as ancillary services which share the tax treatment of the principal service.
* **Application:** For a traveler, the "extra baggage" is not a separate service they want to buy independently; it is an intrinsic part of their ability to use the flight (the transport) to reach their destination. Therefore, it is "absorbed" into the main transport service.

### 2. The Dutch "Baggage = Transport" Definition (Current Decree)

The specific administrative proof that baggage is part of "passenger transport" is found in the current decree from the Ministry of Finance.

* **Current Decree:** **Besluit nr. 2026-1395** (titled: *Besluit Toelichting Tabel II bij de Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968*), effective since **February 28, 2026**.
* **Specific Article:** **Toelichting op Post b.3**.
* **The Provision:** This decree explicitly confirms that "the service performed regarding baggage carried by the passengers" is included under the zero-rate (0%) application for international passenger transport (Post b.3).
* **Why it matters:** This decree provides the legal certainty that the Dutch Tax Authorities (Belastingdienst) view baggage as an inseparable part of the international transport service mentioned in the Law.

### 3. Our Role as Intermediary (Legal Reference for the 0% Rate)

As an intermediary, our mediation fee (including any markup) also qualifies for the 0% rate because it relates to a zero-rated international transport service.

* **Legal Reference:** **Wet op de omzetbelasting 1968, Tabel II, onderdeel b, post 4**.
* **The Provision:** This post applies the **0% rate** to "services by intermediaries acting in the name and for the account of another, provided they relate to the performances referred to in posts b.1 through b.3."
* **Connection:** Because extra baggage is legally defined as part of the performance in **Post b.3** (International Transport), your mediation of that baggage is covered by **Post b.4**.
